Valuation Concentration and Positive Price Skewness

Affordable Price Concentration: Over 75% of all trading sessions take place below PKR 480, and the median price is PKR 134.84. This demonstrates that the majority of listed companies are priced accessibly for domestic retail participants.

Why Mean Exceeds Median: The mathematical mean is PKR 856.77 because a small cohort of high-denomination multinational corporations (such as Unilever at PKR 34,000 and Rafhan Maize at PKR 12,000) raises the arithmetic average. This positive skewness (+6.88) is standard in equity distributions, where asset prices are bounded by zero on the downside but have unbounded upside potential.

Capital Density and Sectoral Volume Concentration

Analytical Findings and Market Interpretation
Economic Reality of Outliers: Why Nominal High Prices Are Authentic

Share Count Capital Structure: A nominal share price equals total equity valuation divided by the number of issued shares. Companies like K-Electric have over 27 billion shares, resulting in a unit price between PKR 1.57 and PKR 6.20. In contrast, Unilever Pakistan Foods has very few outstanding shares and has never executed a stock split, producing a unit price of up to PKR 34,139.55.

Why Outliers Must Not Be Removed: Removing or capping these high-priced stocks at statistical thresholds would artificially erase 97% of Unilever's corporate valuation. In quantitative financial analysis, outlier detection must evaluate percentage returns rather than raw nominal share prices across heterogeneous instruments.

Macroeconomic Phases: IMF Stabilization and Structural Re-Rating

2021 to Mid-2023 Consolidation: Market performance was constrained by high inflation, currency devaluation, and monetary policy tightening. Most equities moved sideways or drifted lower during this interval.

July 2023 IMF Catalyst and Bull Market: Approval of the $3 Billion IMF Stand-By Arrangement in July 2023 eliminated sovereign default risks and catalyzed a major structural advance. Core bellwethers like Systems Limited, Hub Power, and Lucky Cement reached between 250% and 350% of their 2021 base values.

Multicollinearity and Price-Volume Elasticity

Near-Unity Price Multicollinearity: Daily High and Closing Price exhibit a near-perfect correlation (+0.9997). In predictive modeling, raw price components should be converted into percentage returns to eliminate multicollinearity.

Volume-Price Decoupling: The correlation between closing price and traded volume is near zero (-0.0630), as volume expands during both market panics and breakout rallies. Conversely, volume correlates positively with intraday volatility (+0.1340), confirming that wider price dispersion attracts greater trading activity.
